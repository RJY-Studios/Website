Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

RJY Studios LLC ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we handle it on our website, rjystudios.com, and in emails you send us. It covers what we collect, how we collect and use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your personal data.

Information we collect

  • When you email us: your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write in your message.
  • When you visit this website: like any website, the services that deliver it receive your IP address and basic browser details (such as browser type and the page you asked for) so they can send you the page. We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we don't ask you to sign in.
  • Your light or dark mode choice: if you use the light and dark switch, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

How we use information

We use your email only to reply and keep a record of our conversation. Your IP address and browser details are used only to deliver the site and keep it secure. We never sell your information or use it for advertising or profiling.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the companies that help run this website and our email.

  • GitHub: hosts this website and may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts: provides the site's fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when your browser loads them.
  • Squarespace: manages our domain name.
  • Titan Email: delivers and stores the emails you send us.

These companies receive only what they need, such as your IP address or your email, and protect it at least as well as this policy does. We may also share information when required by law or as part of a business transfer such as a merger.

How long we keep it

  • Your emails: kept only as long as we need them to reply and keep a record of our conversation, or deleted sooner if you ask.
  • Server logs: kept by GitHub under its own rules. We don't keep copies.
  • Your light or dark mode choice: stays in your browser until you clear this site's data.

Managing your information

At any time, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop using it. Contact us and we'll take care of it and let you know when it's done. To reset your light or dark mode choice, clear this site's data in your browser.

Security

We take care to keep your information safe. This website is served over a secure connection, and we hold only the little information described in this policy.

Children

This website is meant for adults, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has contacted us, let us know and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information. Email us and we'll help, and you'll never be treated differently for asking.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
